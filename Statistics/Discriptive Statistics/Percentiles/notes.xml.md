# Percentiles

A **percentile** is a measure of position that tells us where a value
stands relative to the other values in an ordered dataset.

A percentile divides an ordered dataset into **100 parts**.

For example:

- $P_{10}$ = 10th percentile
- $P_{25}$ = 25th percentile
- $P_{50}$ = 50th percentile
- $P_{75}$ = 75th percentile
- $P_{90}$ = 90th percentile
- $P_{99}$ = 99th percentile

---

# 1. What Does a Percentile Mean?

If a value is at the **80th percentile**, it means that approximately
80% of the reference observations are at or below that value,
depending on the percentile definition used.

### Example

Suppose a student's score is at the 90th percentile.

This means the student's score is higher than or equal to the scores
of approximately 90% of the reference group.

> Percentile describes **relative position**, not percentage marks.

---

# 2. Percentile vs Percentage

These terms are different.

### Percentage

Percentage tells us how much of a quantity is expressed out of 100.

Example:

If a student gets 80 marks out of 100:

$$
Percentage = 80\%
$$

### Percentile

Percentile describes the relative position of a value in a dataset.

Example:

A student can have:

$$
Marks = 80\%
$$

but:

$$
Percentile = 92
$$

These are completely different concepts.

| $P_{95}$ | 95th percentile | - |
| $P_{99}$ | 99th percentile | - |

---

# 4. Relationship Between Percentiles and Quartiles

Quartiles divide ordered data into four sections.

Therefore:

$$
Q_1=P_{25}
$$

$$
Q_2=P_{50}=Median
$$

$$
Q_3=P_{75}
$$
